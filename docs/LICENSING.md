# Licensing from LocalSave 2.7.0

New releases use the custom **LocalSave Personal Use License 1.0** in the root
LICENSE file. It covers the new material subject to its prior-license clause.
The installer displays it for acceptance and installs LICENSE.txt; the app
embeds it and exposes a scrollable viewer under Privacy & help.

## Permitted free use

An individual may use it for their own personal, noncommercial documents,
private study, personal coursework and hobby projects. Personal noncommercial
modification and free sharing are permitted under the terms and notice requirements.

## Excluded uses under the new license

Company or other organizational deployment, employment work, business operations,
paid freelance/client work and commercial projects are excluded. Schools,
nonprofits and government bodies receive no organizational-use grant. An employee
or student may use it privately for permitted personal activities.

This is a licensing policy, not a company-detection or activation system. It
does not add telemetry, accounts or license-key enforcement. The custom terms
have not undergone legal review; they should be reviewed before relying on them
for a licensing dispute or a commercial licensing program.

## Previous MIT grants

Previously released MIT copies and material retain their permissions, including
commercial use under MIT. The preserved notice is in licenses/MIT-PREVIOUS.txt
and included in the new LICENSE as a prior-material notice. The new license
does not withdraw those rights or turn unchanged previously MIT code into
exclusively personal-use code. Anyone may continue using an authorized older
MIT release or covered material under its existing terms.

Keep historical MIT releases and source history accurately labeled. The public
GitHub 2.2.0 MIT release must not be described as retroactively restricted. Historical source and release downloads remain available with their original terms.

## Naming

Use "free for personal, noncommercial use" and "source available" for the new
restricted releases. Do not describe this custom license as MIT, PolyForm or
OSI-approved open source. Keep separate third-party licenses intact.

[Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd).
